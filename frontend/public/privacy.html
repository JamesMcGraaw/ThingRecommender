Privacy Policy

ThingRecommender is a small personal project for tracking recommendations between friends. This page explains what data it collects and how it's used.

What's collected

When you sign in with Google, ThingRecommender receives your name, email address, and Google account ID from Google — just enough to identify you and let people recommend things to you by email. No other Google account data is accessed.

Beyond that, the app stores whatever you enter yourself: recommendations you send or log, ratings you give, and the names/notes you attach to them.

How it's used

Solely to run the app: matching recommendations to accounts, showing you what's been recommended to you, and computing the recommendation-strength scores the app is built around. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything beyond that.

Where it's stored

In a Postgres database hosted by Neon. The app itself runs on Render. Recommended titles may be looked up on TMDB to attach a link, using only the title and media type you enter.

Deleting your data

Since this is a small project without a self-serve delete option yet, email james.mcgraw.allen@gmail.com and it'll be removed.
